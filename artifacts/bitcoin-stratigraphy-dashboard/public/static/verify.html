What a pass does not prove

Read before relying
  • Key trust is yours. Independently pin current and historical issuer keys. Fingerprint selection supports rotation but does not certify a key's provenance or continued safety.
  • Identity is server-recorded. This page does not independently re-verify the original Schnorr-signed NIP-98 event. A valid optional CRL establishes signed registry state at its timestamp; it cannot reveal later revocations or authorize a live request. Without a CRL, revocation is not checked. Freshness depends on your browser clock.
  • Merkle validity is local. A valid inclusion proof does not prove the thermodynamic truth of source data, Bitcoin anchoring of the root, or legal / regulatory compliance.
